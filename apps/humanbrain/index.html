Support for HumanBrain

Explore brain anatomy in a native iPad workspace.

HumanBrain brings interactive 3D brain structures, bilingual search, anatomical descriptions, and reference links to iPad. Requires iPadOS 18 or later.

Explore the model

  • Drag with one finger to rotate. Pinch to zoom, or drag with two fingers to pan. Double-tap to reset the camera.
  • Open the sidebar to browse seven anatomical systems. Search by English name, Chinese name, or region code.
  • Tap a structure to select it, then open Details for anatomy, function, and references.
  • Use Display to change cortex opacity or isolate a selected region.
  • Use Section to explore sagittal, horizontal, and coronal planes. Move the slider to adjust the cut.
  • Use View for observation angles, reset, gesture help, support, and privacy links.

Common questions

Does it work offline? Yes. The brain models and anatomical text are included in the app. Opening external references or this support website requires an internet connection.

Do I need an account? No. HumanBrain has no registration, subscription, advertisements, or in-app purchases.

How do I change the language or appearance? HumanBrain follows iPadOS language preferences and system light or dark appearance. English and Simplified Chinese are supported.

The model looks empty or cut away. Choose View → Reset View. Disable Section or Isolate Selection if you enabled them.

Is this a medical device? HumanBrain is an educational anatomy reference. It does not diagnose conditions or provide treatment recommendations.

操作指南

  • 单指拖动旋转，双指捏合缩放，双指拖动平移；双击模型恢复观察位置。
  • 打开侧栏浏览七大解剖系统，可按中文、英文或脑区编号搜索。
  • 轻点结构选中脑区，再打开详情查看结构、功能与参考文献。
  • 在“显示”中调整皮层不透明度，或单独显示已选脑区。
  • 在“剖切”中切换矢状面、水平面和冠状面，用滑杆调整切面位置。
  • 在“视角”中切换观察方向、重置视图、查看手势帮助、技术支持和隐私政策。

常见问题

能否离线使用？可以。模型和解剖文字随 App 提供；访问外部文献或支持网站时需要网络。

是否需要账号或订阅？不需要。App 无注册、订阅、广告和应用内购买。

如何切换语言和外观？跟随 iPadOS 的语言偏好与系统深浅色外观，支持英文和简体中文。

模型看不见或被切开了？选择“视角 → 重置视图”，并检查是否启用了“剖切”或“单独显示选区”。

可以用于医疗诊断吗？本 App 是解剖学习参考工具，不提供疾病诊断或治疗建议。

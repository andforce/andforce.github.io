把年少的江湖，重新握在手里。

一百单八将，一人一张卡。掌上梁山是一款 iPhone 水浒英雄卡牌收藏与图录应用。

  • 拆包结识好汉，收藏 108 位水浒人物。
  • 翻阅卡牌正反面、小传、人物六视与兵器坐骑图录。
  • 每日识一人，把喜欢的好汉加入心选。
  • 拖动或倾斜手机把玩卡堆，分享人物与收藏海报。
  • 图录可自由浏览；登录后同步收藏，已下载内容可离线欣赏。

收藏保存在哪里？

收藏及沙漏、聚义印保存在云端账户。请使用 Apple 或中国大陆 +86 手机号验证码登录；换设备时使用同一登录方式和账户即可恢复。删除 App 不会删除云端资产；删除账户请进入首页菜单 → 我的账户 → 删除账户。

免费卡包与内购如何使用？

首次登录获得 3 包，每 8 小时免费恢复 1 包，上限 6 包。每包 3 张，普通位置闪卡概率 10%，连续 4 包无闪时第 5 包至少 1 张。每包获得 1 枚聚义印，5 枚可兑换指定普卡，12 枚兑换指定闪卡。详细概率可在应用内查看。

可选购 16、56、120 或 208 个聚义沙漏，每个缩短 1 小时等待。购买由 Apple 处理，到账后不会自动拆包。沙漏只在免费额度用完且您确认后使用。免费与加速卡包规则一致，不提供提现、交易或现金奖励。

购买后没有到账？

请保持联网，确认已登录购买时使用的业务账户，重新打开 App 或在账户页刷新。不要重复购买来重试。若仍未恢复，请邮件提供 App 版本、商品档位和 Apple 订单编号；请勿发送密码或完整付款资料。退款请通过 Apple 购买支持申请。

卡牌为何不随手机倾斜？

请查看系统「减弱动态效果」设置。动作感应需要支持的真机；模拟器中可通过拖动体验卡堆。

拆包、心选或图片遇到问题？

请尝试重新打开应用，并通过邮件提供 App 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和复现步骤。您可自愿附上截图，请先移除私人内容。

Water Margin heroes, collected in your hand.

Liangshan is an iPhone app for collecting 108 Water Margin hero cards and exploring character artwork. The app interface and character text are in Simplified Chinese.

  • Open packs, collect heroes and mark favorites.
  • View card fronts and backs, biographies and character artwork.
  • Meet a featured hero each day.
  • Interact with the card stack using touch or device tilt and share posters.
  • Browse bundled content offline without an account.

Collection storage

Your collection, hourglasses and seals are stored in your cloud account. Sign in with Apple or a mainland China +86 phone verification code. Use the same account and sign-in method on another device to restore assets. Uninstalling the app does not delete the cloud account. To delete it, open the home menu → 我的账户 (My Account) → 删除账户 (Delete Account).

Free packs and purchases

New accounts receive 3 packs. One free pack recovers every 8 hours, up to 6. Packs contain 3 cards, with a 10% base foil chance per position; after 4 packs without a foil, the fifth guarantees at least one. Each pack awards one seal; exchange 5 for a chosen normal card or 12 for a chosen foil card. Full odds are available in the app.

Optional consumable purchases provide 16, 56, 120 or 208 hourglasses. Each removes one hour of waiting. Apple processes payments; buying does not automatically open packs. Hourglasses are used only when free packs are exhausted and you confirm. Free and accelerated packs use identical rules. There is no cash-out, trading or cash reward.

Missing purchased hourglasses?

Stay online, sign in to the same app account used for the purchase, and reopen the app or refresh My Account. Do not repurchase to retry. Contact support with the app version, product tier and Apple order ID if needed; never send passwords or full payment details. Request refunds through Apple purchase support.

Motion effects

Check the system Reduce Motion setting. Device tilt requires a supported physical device; the card stack also supports touch dragging.

Contact support

Email the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps to reproduce your issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information before sending.

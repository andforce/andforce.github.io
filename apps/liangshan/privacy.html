您的隐私与掌上梁山

本政策适用于王迪远开发的 iPhone 应用「水浒」（应用内名称「掌上梁山」，Bundle ID：com.wdyrix.shuihu）。应用用于浏览、收藏和分享水浒英雄卡牌与人物图录，核心功能可离线使用。

应用不收集个人数据

掌上梁山不要求注册或登录，不设开发者运营的服务器，不集成广告、第三方分析或跟踪 SDK。应用不会主动向开发者上传个人信息、设备标识、搜索内容、收藏记录或浏览行为，也不会跨应用或网站跟踪您。

设备本地保存的信息

已收藏卡牌、心选人物、最近拆包结果、拆包次数、每日领取记录和触感设置保存在设备上的应用存储中。搜索和图录浏览在本机完成。若发现存档损坏，应用可能在本机保留一份损坏记录，以便排查；该记录不会自动上传。

本应用没有应用级 iCloud 同步功能。iOS 可能根据您自己的系统设置备份或恢复应用数据，这由 Apple 的备份服务与相应政策管理。

动作感应与系统权限

把玩卡堆时，应用可使用 Core Motion 提供的实时设备倾斜数据来调整动画。数据仅在本机即时处理，不保存、不上传；卡堆停止显示或应用暂停时停止相关更新，系统「减弱动态效果」设置也会影响这些动画。应用不读取您的联系人、位置、相机、麦克风、健康记录或照片图库。

由您主动发起的分享

您可以使用 iOS 分享面板导出人物或收藏海报。海报包含所选卡牌、人物信息和可能显示的收藏数量。只有在您选择接收对象或其他应用并完成分享后，相应内容才会交给该对象或应用处理；其数据处理适用各自的政策。开发者不会自动收到这些分享内容。

网站、外部链接与邮件支持

您打开隐私政策或支持链接时，会访问本网站。本网站托管在 GitHub Pages；GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址。详见 GitHub Pages 说明和 GitHub 隐私声明。掌上梁山的政策与支持页面不添加分析脚本、广告或 Cookie。

如果您主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件正文和自愿提供的附件，仅用于回复和排查问题，不用于广告或出售。邮件服务商会为传递和保存邮件处理这些信息。请勿在邮件中发送密码或其他不必要的敏感信息。

保存期限、删除与您的选择

本地收藏数据会保留至您删除应用，具体删除及恢复行为受 iOS 备份设置影响。开发者无法访问、恢复或远程删除您设备上的收藏。您可通过下方邮箱请求删除支持邮件；除处理请求或适用义务所需的保留外，我们会删除相关邮件。支持往来仅在解决问题所需期间及适用义务要求的期间保留。

儿童与政策更新

应用不主动收集儿童或其他用户的个人数据。如将来改变数据处理方式，我们会更新本页面、生效日期及适用的 App Store 隐私说明。

Your privacy in Liangshan

This policy applies to 水浒, an iPhone app developed by Diyuan Wang, branded in the app as 掌上梁山 (Liangshan), with bundle ID com.wdyrix.shuihu. The app lets you browse, collect and share Water Margin hero cards and character artwork. Its core features work offline.

No personal data collected by the app

The app requires no account or sign-in, has no developer-operated server, and includes no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not automatically upload personal information, device identifiers, searches, collection records or browsing activity to the developer, or track you across apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

Collected cards, favorites, the latest pack, pack counts, daily-claim records and haptic preferences are stored in the app's local storage. Searching and browsing happen on the device. If a saved record is damaged, a recovery copy may be retained locally; it is not automatically uploaded.

There is no app-level iCloud synchronization. iOS may back up or restore app data according to your system settings, under Apple's backup services and policies.

Motion and system access

The interactive card stack may use real-time device tilt from Core Motion to animate the cards. This data is processed on the device without retention or upload. Relevant updates stop when the card stack is no longer active or the app pauses; the system's Reduce Motion setting also affects these animations. The app does not read contacts, location, camera, microphone, health records or your photo library.

Sharing you initiate

You can export a hero or collection poster through the iOS share sheet. A poster contains selected artwork, character information and, where shown, your collection count. Content is passed to the destination you choose only when you complete sharing. That destination's privacy policy applies. The developer does not automatically receive shared posters.

This website, external links and support email

Opening a privacy or support link visits this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. These Liangshan pages add no analytics scripts, advertisements or cookies.

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message and voluntary attachments. They are used only to respond and investigate issues, and are not sold or used for advertising. Email providers process correspondence for delivery and storage. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention, deletion and choices

Your collection stays on your device until the app is deleted, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior. The developer cannot access, restore or remotely delete that collection. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence at the email below; we will delete it except where retention is needed to handle your request or meet applicable obligations.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If its data practices change, this page, its effective date and the applicable App Store privacy details will be updated.
